### Protective Taping

#### Applying tape

To protect the IMU that will be tightly stacked on top of the rest of our electronics we want to apply Kapton Tape to the back of the IMU
to ensure that no contacts are shorted against the board underneath it

### Magnetometer soldering

The LSM6DSV chip has its magnetometer disabled out of the box through jumper pins, if you plan on using them you will need to connect these pins.
If you don't want to use the magnetometer you can skip this step.

:::note
The power drain from connecting the magnetometer is negligible and can be freely enabled and disabled in software with a single if it doesn't work well for you so I recommend connecting it anyway.

For more information on the difference between using a magnetometer (9 DOF) and not (6 DOF) you can continue reading [Here](https://docs.slimevr.dev/diy/imu-comparison.html#whats-the-difference-between-an-imu-with-a-magnetometer-9-dof-and-an-imu-without-a-magnetometer-6-dof)

Some important snips from the above documentation:

> "IMUs with a magnetometer, like a compass, use the Earth's magnetic field as a reference point to reduce gyroscope drift, however they require a stable magnetic environment or else they will perform erratically."

> "IMUs without a magnetometer don't require a stable magnetic environment, but are prone to gyroscope drift over time due to being unable to differentiate sensor noise from actual movement."

> "For SlimeVR's purposes, neither is inherently better or worse than the other."

:::

To connect the magnetometer you must bridge pads 5, 6, 7, and 8 like so:

### Pre-Soldering the IMU

This step has been designed by [ibis-trackers](https://github.com/VYRO-VR/ibis-trackers) to avoid damaging other components when connecting your IMU. This step uses
the [Soldering Cube](/models/SolderCube.stl) model to assist with aligning and soldering pins.

#### Preparing Pins

Your MCU board should have come with set of pins, we will be using these to connect our MCU and IMU together.

- B+ & GND - Battery terminals
- 006 & GND - Button

## Wire mod

### Cutting wire

Next to improve the signal strength of our MCU ("SuperMinis have horrendous signal strength by themselves") we will cut a piece of wire to act as a antenna.

Ensure you solder the wires angled to the left side of the MCU as shown in the photo as this is where the battery will sit within the case and your pins will not fit / will snap due to case clearance if you solder them to the right
