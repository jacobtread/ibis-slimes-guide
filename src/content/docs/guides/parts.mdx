:::note
For IMUs and MCUs it's recommended that you buy at least 20% more than you plan to use in case you break some components during assembly

For example:

If you're building 8 trackers purchase 10 IMUs and 10 MCUs

If you are more experienced at soldering small components your chance of breaking components is much lower and you can probably skip buying additional MCUs however its
still a good idea to purchase additional IMUs as the magnetometer is sensitive to heat
and can be broken if you apply too much heat for too long
:::

:::note
Alternative IMUs can be used however the pins you are required to solder may differ from the guide so using another IMU is at your own discretion.

Moffshop is a trusted seller of LSM6DSV within the SlimeVR community so they are the
recommended source for these chips. It is not recommended **NOT** buy your IMU from AliExpress as this is a important component and AliExpress quality can vary greatly
for this.
:::

## Printed components

Some components that you'll likely need to 3D print or have 3D printed for you if you do not own a 3D printer:

- [Solder Cube](/models/SolderCube.stl) - Small printed holder ot assist with soldering your IMU
- [Body](/models/tracker/Body.stl) - Body of the tracker case
- [Lid](/models/tracker/Lid.stl) - Lid to the tracker case
- [Tray](/models/tracker/Tray.stl) - Tray that the trackers will slide into (Not required for VYRO straps as they come included)
- [GoProChestMount](/models/tracker/GoProChestMount.stl) - Go pro strap chest mount (Only required if you are making your own straps that use a Go Pro chest mount)
