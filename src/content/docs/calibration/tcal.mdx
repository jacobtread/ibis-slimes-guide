## What

Temperature Calibration is a type of calibration which compensates for temperature-related gyro drift. As temperature changes it can affect the gyroscope and cause tracker drift, this method
of calibration involves gradually heating up the trackers while the trackers automatically collect data, this data is then used to create a temperature curve that is used to compensate for the
temperature-related gyro drift.

This is performed by cooling and then subsequently heating the trackers. The trackers will capture data as long as they detect themselves heating up while stationary

## Requirements

- Fridge
- 3D Printer (Optimal for easy heating, a hot water bottle + towel or a hairdryer can be used as an alternative if unavailable)

<Steps>

1. In NRF-Connect or within the SlimeVR app serial console run the `send all tcal on` command to your receiver, this will tell all your trackers to enable temperature calibration.

    You can find the serial console in the SlimeVR server here:

    <Image
        src={serialConsole}
        alt="serial console"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

    You will need to confirm you know what you are doing:

    <Image
        src={serialConsoleConfirmSend}
        alt="serial console confirm send"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

    Then you can run the command:

    <Image
        src={sendAllTcalOn}
        alt="send all tcal on"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

2. Next send the `send all tcal auto on` command to your receiver, this will tell all your connected trackers to begin automatic calibration

    <Image
        src={sendAllTcalAutoOn}
        alt="send all tcal on"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

3. Ensure your trackers are at room temperature, you can assume they are likely already at room temperature if you haven't heated them

    <Image
        src={slimesRoomTemp}
        alt="send all tcal on"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

4. Place all of your trackers in your fridge, observe the temperature of your trackers in the SlimeVR server until they reach as close to 5°C as possible (8-10°C is acceptable if you are unable to get the trackers any colder)

    Ensure there is adequate space between your trackers so that they can cool down faster:

    <Image
        src={tcalFridge}
        alt="tcal fridge"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

    <Image
        src={slimesFridgeTemp}
        alt="send all tcal on"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

5. Once the trackers have settled at the desired temperature take the trackers out of your fridge and place them directly on the bed of your 3D printer (Do not start heating the bed yet), allow your trackers to reach room temperature before continuing

    <Image
        src={tcalPrinterBed}
        alt="send all tcal on"
        style="max-height: 500px;width:auto;"
    />

6. After your trackers have reached room temperature you can gradually raise the temperature of your printer bed in small increments to allow the trackers to slowly heat up to 45°C

    <Image
        src={slimesRoomTemp}
        alt="send all tcal on"
        style="max-height: 300px;width:auto;"
    />

7. Once the trackers have settled for a few moments at 45°C take your trackers out of your printer and allow them to return to room temperature

    <Aside type="danger">
        Do NOT leave your trackers unattended on your heated 3D printer bed
        prolonged heat exposure can damage your battery or cause it to swell.
    </Aside>

8. Finally send the `send all tcal auto off` command to your receiver, this will disable automatic temperature calibration now that we have collected all the required data.

</Steps>
